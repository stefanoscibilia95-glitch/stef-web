Research profile

I am a political scientist working on European Union economic governance and the domestic politics of socio-economic reform in EU Member States.

Research interests: EU governance, comparative political economy, social policy, case study research

Academic appointments

2023–present
PhD Candidate in Political Science, Policy, Politics, and Society, Erasmus School of Social and Behavioural Sciences, Erasmus University Rotterdam.
  • Doctoral research is focused on understanding how domestic politics within Member States affect the Europeanization of socio-economic policy. Conducted in-depth process tracing analyses of labour market and pension reforms in Italy and Spain, with a particular focus on the “domestic effect” of the European Semester, the Recovery and Resilience Facility, and the Stability and Growth Pact in the 2011–2025 period.
  • Part of the research project Towards a resilient European Union? Post-pandemic economic governance amidst politicisation (PEGAP), funded by the Dutch Research Council (NWO).
  • Supervisors: prof. dr. Markus Haverland (main supervisor and PEGAP principal investigator), dr. Asya Zhelyazkova, dr. Pieter Tuytens.
